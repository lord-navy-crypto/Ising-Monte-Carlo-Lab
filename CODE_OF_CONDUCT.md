# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-focused. Critique code, numerical methods, assumptions, and results rather than people. Scientific disagreement is welcome when it is expressed constructively and supported by reproducible reasoning or evidence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, or publication of another person's private information are not acceptable. Repository maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.
